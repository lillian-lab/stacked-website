The Problem

The raise is broken at every step.

Founders are over-spending, under-informed, and flying blind through the most important process of their company's life.

01

Materials scattered across five platforms

You are already stretched thin. The last thing you need is to hunt down every document that mentions your runway every time a number changes. Founders spend hours each week just keeping files consistent across tools that were never built to talk to each other.

02

Founders don't know what investors actually want

Critical due diligence information gets missed until it's too late. The gap between what founders present and what investors need is where rounds die.

03

Data rooms are a black box

Once you share sensitive documents, all visibility disappears. There is no insight into where investors spend their time, what they share, or what questions are forming.

04

Warm intros require manual detective work

The only reliable path to investors requires manually combing through LinkedIn hoping to spot a mutual connection that might open a door.

05

Founders pay $10–20K for what should be built in

Design and investor relations are not a founder's specialty. They hire expensive consultants for work that a purpose-built platform should handle by default.

The Solution

One OS. Every stage of the raise.

Stacked consolidates every raising tool into one connected platform. Pitch deck, data room, cap table, and CRM all talking to each other, updated from one place.

Dynamic Content Layer

Update one metric and watch it propagate instantly across every investor-facing document. One source of truth, zero version drift.

Smart Data Room

Custom trackable links per investor. See exactly where they spend time, what they revisit, and what questions are forming before they ask them.

Investor CRM

Replace the spreadsheet. Track every investor from first touch to committed capital. Surface warm intro paths through your network automatically.

Cap Table Management

Built for pre-seed simplicity and SAFE-native from day one. Designed to scale alongside you through Seed and beyond.
