Not a spreadsheet. Not a sales CRM stretched to fit. Two tools built around what a raise actually looks like.

Live end of 2026

The Almanac

The tool that tells you who is actually likely to invest. The Almanac reviews an investor's past deals and shows you the startups they have actually backed, so you stop guessing or wasting time meeting with a firm that was never going to invest in you.

  • Claims versus reality on every firm
  • Stage, check size, and sector fit at a glance
  • Sourced, not guessed at
Live early 2027

Founder CRM

A CRM that actually understands what a raise looks like. Integrated with your email and your meeting notetaker, it automatically pulls the small tasks that get lost and holds you accountable to what you have promised.

Why I'm building this.

I learned about startups and the VC world in high school. I loved the idea of being an angel or a VC, empowering founders with new ideas to build the next Google or Amazon.

I was so eager that I did not wait until I had a great idea to start a company. I opened an ecommerce business as a junior in high school, just to get a taste of entrepreneurship and discover what it feels like to build a brand new company.

I joined Bearcat Ventures, the University of Cincinnati's student VC fund, my freshman year, because I was excited to be at the forefront of discovering new innovations, even as a student. This platform connects back to my passion of helping people build the next big thing.

Three lenses on the same problem.

The VC lens
General Partner at Bearcat Ventures, University of Cincinnati's student run pre-seed fund.
The operator lens
BizOps intern at Narratize, a venture backed startup, mid raise.
The builder lens
Founder of Sultana's Cases, a direct to consumer phone case brand.

I want founders in the room while I build this. I want to hear what's actually not working for you, and help make fundraising a little easier. A little less of a headache.
